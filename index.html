The course

From geometric intuition to learning systems.

CSE 6740 develops the foundations and practical tools needed to reason about machine learning and data analysis. We move from clustering and dimensionality reduction through statistical learning, neural networks, generative models, and reinforcement learning.

This site is the shared memory of the class: lecture notes, examples, references, and refinements contributed by students and reviewed by the course team.

Learning pathway

One semester, four arcs

01

Discover structure

Clustering, PCA, density estimation, mixture models, and EM.

02

Learn to predict

Feature selection, trees, classifiers, kernels, and Gaussian processes.

03

Build deep models

Optimization, neural networks, CNNs, autoencoders, and diffusion.

04

Act & sequence

Recurrent models, transformers, and reinforcement learning.
