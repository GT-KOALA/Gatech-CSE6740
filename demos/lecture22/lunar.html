Lunar landing

Ready for flight

Flight controls

Tap A to tip right or D to tip left. Fire the opposite jet to stop the rotation. Hold W to thrust in the direction the nose points.

Touchdown

Bring both feet over the green pad. Keep sideways speed below 0.6 m/s, vertical speed below 1 m/s, tilt within 12° and rotation within 15°/s.

The autopilot tilts to cancel drift, then levels the craft and brakes its descent.
