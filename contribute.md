<section class="page-intro contribute-intro">
  <p class="eyebrow">Student collaboration</p>
  <h1>Help write the course we learn together.</h1>
  <p>Each lecture is supported by four students. The goal is not a transcript—it is a clear, accurate resource that helps a future reader understand the material.</p>
</section>

<section class="contribution-steps">
  <article><span>01</span><h2>Claim a lecture</h2><p>Open the lecture’s sign-up issue and comment with the role you would like. A maintainer adds your GitHub name to the page.</p></article>
  <article><span>02</span><h2>Choose a role</h2><p><strong>Notes editor</strong>, <strong>example builder</strong>, <strong>visual explainer</strong>, or <strong>reference checker</strong>. Coordinate with your three teammates.</p></article>
  <article><span>03</span><h2>Open a pull request</h2><p>Edit the Markdown page in <code>_lectures/</code>, preview your work, and submit it for instructor review.</p></article>
</section>

<section class="quality-grid">
  <div><p class="eyebrow">A strong contribution</p><h2>Make the idea easier to see.</h2></div>
  <ul>
    <li>Explains concepts in your own words</li>
    <li>Checks equations, code, claims, and links</li>
    <li>Uses accessible labels and descriptive alt text</li>
    <li>Cites sources and respects licenses</li>
    <li>Contains no private, graded, or assessment material</li>
  </ul>
</section>
